Privacy

How privacy works

Privacy by default. GPS is opt-in. Raw vehicle data stays on your device, and only the features a report needs are uploaded.

  • GPS stays off

    Location is off until you turn it on. Leaving it off means the report has no GPS track.

  • Raw stays local

    Raw readings stay on your device.

  • Only what the report needs

    A report uploads the features behind a claim, not a raw log of the trip.

FAQ

Before you join

Is this ChatGPT?

No. Vehicle data comes first. Garumi reports from the car. It is not a chatbot that guesses from symptoms.

Is GPS on?

No. GPS is off by default. It stays off unless you turn it on.

Is always-on reporting live today?

Not yet. Always-on without opening an app every trip is where we are headed - not what ships as the product today.

Which cars are first?

Early access starts with US owners of the Subaru BRZ, Toyota GR86, Infiniti G37, and Infiniti Q60. If you drive something else, you can still request access and say so.

What does verified or inferred mean?

Verified means the vehicle data supports the claim. Inferred means the claim is a conclusion, and the report labels it that way.

Do you repair the car?

No. Garumi reports from the vehicle. A licensed shop still does inspection and repair.
