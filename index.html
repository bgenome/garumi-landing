VIN profile

Example shape only: 2017 Subaru BRZ, VIN redacted. A real check confirms the model year from the VIN you choose to share and adds a short checklist for that car. This sample does not decode a real VIN.

Symptom check

Likely: glazed front pads or a wear indicator. Cold squeal that eases after a few stops is common. That pattern alone does not mean a caliper has failed.

Less likely until measured: rotors under minimum thickness. A lip or a groove needs a number.

Not supported by this note: a wheel bearing. Bearing noise usually follows road speed. It does not start and stop with the pedal.

Invoice sanity

  • A pad job is the sane first quote when pads are thin and rotors are still in spec.
  • Question a quote that bundles pads, rotors, and calipers for an overnight squeal with no measurements written down.

Parts fitment note

Match front pads to a 2017 Subaru BRZ. A pad listed only for a later Toyota GR86 is not automatically the same part. Do not buy drilled rotors to cure a squeal before someone measures thickness.
